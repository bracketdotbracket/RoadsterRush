# Boost & power-ups

**Three inventories. Different decisions.**

Blue boost canisters, purple mystery boxes and your regenerating jump reserve have separate roles. Collect a racing advantage, save it for the right moment, and keep enough awareness to respond when another driver attacks.

| Resource         | Where it comes from           | What you carry        | How to use it          |
| :--------------- | :---------------------------- | :-------------------- | :--------------------- |
| **Boost**        | Blue canisters on the circuit | Up to three canisters | Tap **Shift** / **RB** |
| **Party item**   | Purple mystery boxes          | One random item       | Tap **E** / **B**      |
| **Jump reserve** | Regenerates over time         | Up to 100 charge      | Tap **Space** / **A**  |

## Blue canisters · ten seconds of boost

Each circuit has **eight boost pickups**. Drive through one to collect it; the pickup becomes available again after **20 seconds**. Racers share pickups, so another driver can reach one before you.

Tap Shift to spend one canister on a **10-second boost**. Release the key once it activates. The car keeps receiving thrust without holding Shift or the accelerator.

An active boost cannot be extended by holding the key. Once it ends, a fresh press can spend the next stored canister. Braking and reverse override the thrust while the timer continues. Off-road boost gives a smaller speed increase to help you return to the circuit.

**Good moment:** a clear straight after you have finished the corner. **Poor moment:** a tight turn where you immediately need to brake away the advantage.

## Purple boxes · one item, one opportunity

The **18 mystery boxes** are arranged in six rows of three. A box returns **10 seconds** after collection. Each pickup gives a random Shield, Shockwave or EMP Trap if your item slot is empty.

Hold the item as long as you need, then tap E to use it. Holding the button will not automatically spend the next item you collect.

### Shield

**DEFEND · BLOCKS ONE ATTACK · FOUR-SECOND WINDOW**

The shield surrounds your car and blocks one power-up hit. If no attack arrives, it expires after **four seconds**.

Activate it before a visible trap or when a nearby rival is in position to attack. It does not stop physical collisions with cars or barriers.

### Shockwave

**ATTACK · 12-METRE RANGE · SHORT SLOWDOWN**

A shockwave pushes nearby rivals away and reduces their acceleration and top speed for **1.2 seconds**. Targets must be within **12 metres** and close enough vertically to be affected.

Use it when another driver is alongside or contesting your line. It is a close-range attack, so firing while rivals are far ahead spends the item without an advantage.

### EMP Trap

**SET A TRAP · DROP BEHIND · INTERRUPT BOOST**

Drop an EMP trap behind the car while grounded on the road. It arms after **0.5 seconds** and remains for up to **10 seconds**, unless a rival triggers it first.

A hit slows the rival and interrupts boost thrust for **1.5 seconds**. It does not remove their stored canisters. If they were already boosting, the boost timer keeps counting down while thrust is interrupted.

Place it when another driver is following your route. Your own traps cannot hit you. Up to **three per driver** and **12 per race** can exist at once.

## Counterplay

| Threat                             | Your response                                                               |
| :--------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **An orange trap ahead**           | Jump over it with enough clearance, steer around it or activate a shield.   |
| **A rival within shockwave range** | Leave space or time your shield for the attack.                             |
| **You have been hit**              | Keep steering and driving. A hit does not take away those controls.         |
| **Your boost is interrupted**      | The timer keeps running; remaining thrust resumes when the EMP effect ends. |

A hit or shield block gives **two seconds of attack protection**, limiting immediate repeated attacks. You can still steer, brake, reverse and jump after a hit.

Jumping over an EMP requires the car’s base to clear it by more than **1.2 metres**. A last-second jump may not be high enough.

> **Keep the inventories separate in your head**
>
> A canister gives speed. An item changes an encounter. Jump reserve gets you over the obstacle. Spending one does not automatically spend the others.
