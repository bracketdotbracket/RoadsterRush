# Your first race

**From the home screen to your first three laps.**

Roadster Rush plays in your desktop browser with a keyboard or compatible controller. Begin in Practice if you want to learn the car before racing other drivers.

## 1. Take your place on the grid

Open **[roadsterrush.xyz](https://roadsterrush.xyz/)**. Choose a driver name and a paint colour on the home screen.

Your name appears on the car’s physical rear registration plate. It supports **up to seven characters**: letters, numbers, spaces and hyphens. Lettering is displayed in uppercase, and the home-screen car previews your changes.

## 2. Choose a circuit

Use the circuit card to select **Neon City** or **Starport Circuit**. Your selection is remembered in your browser, and personal best race times are stored separately for each track.

| Circuit              | Setting                                            | Length          |
| :------------------- | :------------------------------------------------- | :-------------- |
| **Neon City**        | City lights and wet-road reflections at night.     | Approx. 1.01 km |
| **Starport Circuit** | Factory straights, launch pads and a sunset coast. | Approx. 1.30 km |

## 3. Pick your session

**Start Race** puts you against three AI rivals. They can collect boost and use party items, so watch the drivers around you as well as the next corner.

**Practice** gives you a solo three-lap session. Learn the checkpoints, test both camera views and practise a jump without another driver in your path.

**Race with Friends** opens private multiplayer rooms for two to four drivers. See the [multiplayer guide](multiplayer.md) for room codes, readiness and hosting requirements.

## 4. Learn the HUD

| Display                   | What to read                                                  |
| :------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Position**              | Where you sit in the race order.                              |
| **Lap & race time**       | Your current lap out of three and the elapsed race time.      |
| **Best lap**              | Your quickest completed lap in the current session.           |
| **Next checkpoint**       | The next required gate and guidance towards it.               |
| **Minimap**               | The shape of the circuit and the racers’ positions.           |
| **Speed & driving state** | Your speed, with feedback such as drifting or off track.      |
| **Boost canisters**       | Stored canisters, or the remaining time on an active boost.   |
| **Held item**             | Your Shield, Shockwave or EMP Trap, plus active item effects. |
| **Jump reserve**          | Available charge and whether a jump or roll is ready.         |

## 5. Complete the race

Pass the **12 checkpoints in order** and cross the start/finish line in the correct direction. Complete **three laps** to finish. Follow the next-checkpoint guide if you lose your way; driving across a different part of the course will not skip the required sequence.

Drive through blue canisters to store boost, and purple mystery boxes to collect an item. You decide when to spend them.

> **Your first-lap goal**
>
> Complete one clean lap before chasing speed. Practise a controlled drift, clear a jump obstacle and save a boost for a straight where you can use it fully.

## Find a comfortable setup

Start with **Balanced** graphics. Switch to **Low** if your device struggles, or **High** for more detailed reflections when performance allows. Use **C** to switch between chase and hood views, **M** for motor audio, and the on-screen fullscreen control for a larger view.

Mobile touch controls are not included in v0.5.0.
