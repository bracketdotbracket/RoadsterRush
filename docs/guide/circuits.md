# The circuits

**City lights. Launch pads. Two different moods, one race to the line.**

Choose a circuit on the home screen before solo racing or practice. In a private room, the host makes the selection and everyone races the same track.

## 01 / Neon City

**NIGHT CIRCUIT · APPROX. 1.01 KM**

A dense skyline, bright streetlights and cyan track edges frame a night race on reflective tarmac. The city gives the circuit its atmosphere: illuminated windows, trackside signs, grandstands, fencing and a start gantry above the road.

The relatively compact course makes it useful for learning the game’s rhythm. Look beyond the bright road edge to the next corner, give yourself room to land, and collect a boost for the section that follows.

| Circuit notes |                                                        |
| :------------ | :----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Mood**      | Night racing, cool blue light and wet-road reflections |
| **Distance**  | Approximately 1.01 km per lap                          |
| **Format**    | Three laps, 12 ordered checkpoints                     |
| **Obstacles** | Two jump obstacles                                     |
| **Pickups**   | Eight boost canisters and 18 mystery boxes             |

## 02 / Starport Circuit

**COASTAL SPACEPORT · APPROX. 1.30 KM**

Starport winds through a fictional Tesla / SpaceX-inspired campus. A solar-roof factory, charging plaza and assembly hangar give way to rockets, launch pads, a launch tower, mission control and the coast.

Warm sunset lighting, dry asphalt and an ocean horizon distinguish it from Neon City. Use Practice to learn the factory straight and launch-pad sweep before committing your boost to an unfamiliar section.

This is an imagined racing environment inspired by electric vehicles and spaceflight, not a reconstruction of a real site.

| Circuit notes |                                                   |
| :------------ | :------------------------------------------------ |
| **Mood**      | Sunset racing, warm colours and an open coastline |
| **Distance**  | Approximately 1.30 km per lap                     |
| **Format**    | Three laps, 12 ordered checkpoints                |
| **Obstacles** | Two jump obstacles                                |
| **Pickups**   | Eight boost canisters and 18 mystery boxes        |

## What both tracks share

Both circuits use the same car controls, boost inventory and party items. Every checkpoint must be passed in order, and start/finish detection spans the shoulders from barrier to barrier.

Trackside barriers stop ground-level escapes with a small rebound. If you end up off the road through an opening or a jump, reduced-speed driving and reverse let you steer back. F remains an optional checkpoint recovery with a three-second penalty.

Your browser saves your selected circuit and stores personal best race times separately for each one.

> **Learn each circuit on its own terms**
>
> A boost that works after one corner may be wasted before the next. Spend a Practice session finding the places where you can keep the car straight and use the whole burst.
