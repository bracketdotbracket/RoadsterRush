# Driving & stunts

**Learn the line. Add the speed. Make the landing count.**

Roadster Rush combines arcade handling with a circuit you must complete in order. Fast racing comes from the sequence: a controlled corner, a useful pickup, a clear jump and a good exit.

## How a race is won

Every race lasts **three laps**. Each circuit has **12 ordered checkpoints**, and the start/finish line spans the track’s shoulders from barrier to barrier.

A lap counts after you pass the required checkpoints and cross the finish in the correct direction. The checkpoint indicator and minimap help you stay on course. Taking a shortcut across the map does not replace passing those checkpoints.

Your finishing time includes any recovery penalties. Local personal best race times are stored per circuit in your browser.

## Cornering

Use the brake to slow before a tight turn, then steer towards the exit. The car loses some speed naturally when you release the accelerator. You can also brake during a boost, which overrides its thrust while the timer continues.

### Controlled drift

Hold **X + steering** to make the nose turn more sharply as the rear slides. Release X to restore grip and straighten the car for acceleration.

Drift engages only while grounded, on the road, moving forward at sufficient speed and steering into a turn. The HUD displays **DRIFTING** when it is active.

**Try this:** enter a corner without boost, hold a short drift, then release X and activate a saved canister once the exit is clear. Work on the timing before holding a longer slide.

## Thruster jumps

Tap **Space** while grounded to jump. The thrusters give a short upward impulse, and gravity brings the car back to the road. They do not provide sustained flight or hovering.

Both circuits include two jump obstacles. Approach in a controlled line, jump early enough to clear the obstacle and keep steering if the landing needs adjustment.

### Air control

Use **A / D**, the arrow keys or the controller’s left stick to change heading in the air. Air steering remains available during a roll. It is useful for lining up with the road, but you still need room to land.

### A 360 barrel roll

1. Tap Space to jump.
2. Release it, then tap Space again in the air.
3. Steer as the roll starts to choose its direction; neutral input rolls right.
4. Complete the rotation before touchdown for a clean stunt landing.

One jump allows one roll. Holding Space does not repeat either action. Trigger the roll early: a late roll can bring a landing speed penalty.

| Jump reserve              | Amount             |
| :------------------------ | :----------------- |
| **Maximum reserve**       | 100 charge         |
| **Jump cost**             | 24 charge          |
| **Roll cost**             | 6 charge           |
| **Completed 360 landing** | Restores 10 charge |

Reserve regenerates after a short delay, and jumps have a cooldown. It is independent of collected boost canisters.

## Barriers and contact

Roadside barriers and catch fencing stop the car from passing through at road level. Impacts produce a small rebound or a slide along the barrier, depending on the approach. Contact with another car can also move you off your intended line.

Shields protect against power-up attacks, not car contact or barriers. Leave yourself space when defending a position.

## Getting back on the road

Going off track reduces grip and speed. You can still drive, steer and reverse back to the circuit; the car is not permanently stuck.

| Off-road movement     | Approximate speed |
| :-------------------- | :---------------- |
| **Forward**           | 22 km/h           |
| **Reverse**           | 14 km/h           |
| **With active boost** | 32 km/h           |

Use **R** to reverse out of an awkward position. Releasing the accelerator lets the car slow to a stop.

### Recovery

Press **F** to return to your last checkpoint. Recovery adds **three seconds to your final race time** and ends active boost and item effects. Stored boost canisters and an unused held item remain available.

> **Build a clean lap first**
>
> Learn where you brake, where you jump and where you can spend a full boost. Add stunts and attacks once the route feels familiar.
