# v0.5.0 & what’s next

**An early playable release. More builds and updates are on the way.**

Roadster Rush is in active development. This release establishes the core experience: a custom Roadster, two circuits, three-lap races and an arcade toolkit of boost, jumps, drifting and party items.

## Playable in v0.5.0

| Area                 | Included                                                                                                             |
| :------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Circuits**         | Neon City and Starport Circuit, each with ordered checkpoints, jump obstacles and full-width start/finish detection. |
| **Modes**            | Three AI rivals, solo Practice and private rooms for two to four drivers.                                            |
| **Driving**          | Acceleration, braking, reverse, controlled drifting, air steering and off-road recovery.                             |
| **Stunts**           | Contextual Space/A jump and 360 barrel roll, with a regenerating jump reserve.                                       |
| **Boost**            | Collectible canisters, storage for three and ten-second activation.                                                  |
| **Party racing**     | Shield, Shockwave and EMP Trap items, shared pickups and attack protection.                                          |
| **Car presentation** | Paint selection, seven-character rear registration plates, animated wheels, steering, wing and thruster effects.     |
| **Race interface**   | Position, lap timing, checkpoint guidance, minimap, inventories and results.                                         |
| **Settings**         | Keyboard and standard gamepad controls, two camera views, motor audio, fullscreen and graphics presets.              |

## Development direction

More builds will refine the feel of the car and the experience around it. Current areas of attention include:

- **Driving feel:** clearer feedback through corners, jumps, landings and contact.
- **Presentation:** stronger visual consistency and continued work on smooth movement.
- **Performance:** broader testing across browsers, hardware and graphics settings.
- **Multiplayer:** testing the experience under real network conditions and improving reliability.

These are areas for continued improvement, not a dated release schedule. Future additions will evolve with playtesting and feedback.

## Current boundaries

The game is designed for desktop browsers. Mobile touch controls, public matchmaking, accounts, persistent online leaderboards and reconnecting to an ongoing race are not part of this release.

Online rooms depend on a separately running race server and are temporary. Configured connection limits are not a measured capacity guarantee; public hosting needs load testing.

## Give useful feedback

When reporting a problem, include:

1. **Circuit and mode:** Neon City or Starport; solo, Practice or private room.
2. **Your setup:** browser, operating system, graphics setting and keyboard/controller.
3. **What happened:** the action just before the issue and what you expected instead.
4. **Evidence:** a screenshot or short clip, if available.

For a handling suggestion, describe the corner, jump or collision where it felt wrong. For multiplayer, include whether other drivers saw the same issue.
